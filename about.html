About Florin-Ciprian Argatu

Associate Professor, PhD in Electrical Engineering, at the Faculty of Electrical Engineering, National University of Science and Technology POLITEHNICA Bucharest.

Research and teaching

My work connects electrical measurements and instrumentation with practical monitoring systems. My interests include power quality, distributed measurement architectures, industrial IoT and e-Health measurement and monitoring systems.

I teach courses and coordinate practical activities in the Faculties of Electrical Engineering and Medical Engineering, with an emphasis on sensor acquisition, signal processing, embedded systems and measurement applications.
